Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Liberty Bell Digital ("Liberty Bell Digital," "we," "us," or "our") provides AI receptionist, phone answering, and local lead-generation marketing services to small and local businesses, including businesses in the auto repair, home services, restaurant, and barbershop / personal care industries, as well as other local service industries we work with or plan to work with in the future.

This Privacy Policy applies across all Liberty Bell Digital websites and industry-specific landing pages (collectively, the "Sites"). It explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you. It applies to visitors to our Sites, prospective clients who submit an inquiry or booking form, and individuals whose calls or messages are handled by an AI receptionist we operate on behalf of one of our client businesses.

1. Who We Are and Who This Policy Covers

Liberty Bell Digital is a marketing and business-automation agency. We build websites, run local advertising, and operate AI phone/SMS receptionist systems for local businesses across several industries, currently including:

  • Auto repair shops
  • Home services companies (e.g. HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and similar trades)
  • Restaurants and food-service businesses
  • Barbershops and personal-care businesses

We may add additional local-service industries over time; when we do, this Policy continues to apply to those industries as well, and this single Policy governs all of our Sites regardless of industry.

This Policy covers two categories of people: (a) visitors and prospective clients who interact with our own marketing Sites (for example, by submitting a "Book My Free Strategy Call" form), and (b) end customers of our client businesses whose calls, texts, or form submissions are handled by an AI receptionist or messaging system we operate on that client's behalf. Where our client is the one who determines how a particular customer's information is used (for example, a restaurant using our AI receptionist to take reservations), that client is responsible for its own privacy practices toward its customers, and we act as a service provider processing that information on the client's behalf and in accordance with this Policy and our agreement with the client.

2. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide to us directly

When you fill out a form on one of our Sites (such as "Book My Free Strategy Call"), we collect information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business name, business type/industry, and website or Google Business Profile link
  • Any details you choose to share about your business or request
  • Your SMS consent choices (see Section 4 below)

b. Information collected through our AI receptionist and calling/texting systems

When our AI receptionist handles a phone call or text on behalf of a client business, we may collect the caller's phone number, the content of the call or message (including recordings or transcripts, where applicable and permitted by law), and details relevant to the request (e.g., reservation time, party size, appointment type, vehicle information, service requested).

c. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our Sites use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use our Sites and to measure and improve our advertising. This may include your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and interactions with our ads on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram). See Section 7 (Cookies & Advertising Technologies) for more detail.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule the strategy call or business review you requested
  • To operate the AI receptionist and messaging services our clients have engaged us to provide (answering calls, capturing reservations/appointments/leads, sending confirmations)
  • To send you SMS text messages and/or place calls, consistent with the consent you provide (see Section 4)
  • To send informational, transactional, and — where you've separately opted in — marketing/promotional communications
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve our Sites and services
  • To measure and optimize our advertising, including through Meta's advertising tools
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

4. SMS / Text Messaging Consent and Practices

Where our booking forms collect a phone number, we present two separate, unchecked consent checkboxes so you can choose what you'd like to receive:

  • Non-marketing (transactional/informational) messages — such as confirmations, follow-ups, or responses related to a request you made.
  • Marketing and promotional messages — such as special offers, discounts, or new-service updates.

Neither checkbox is pre-checked, and checking the marketing checkbox is never required to submit a form or receive service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or HELP for assistance. Your phone number and SMS consent are not sold, and are not shared with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers we use to operate our business and deliver these services, such as our CRM, forms, and messaging platform (GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), telephone and SMS carriers that transmit calls and messages, our website hosting provider (Vercel), and advertising/analytics platforms (Meta).
  • The client business you contacted — if you submit a form or place a call/text intended for one of our client businesses (for example, a restaurant's reservation line), we share the relevant details with that business so it can fulfill your request.
  • Legal and safety reasons — where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Liberty Bell Digital, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, respond to your requests, comply with our legal and carrier/regulatory obligations (including SMS opt-in/opt-out records), and for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention and dispute resolution. When information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Cookies & Advertising Technologies

Our Sites use the Meta Pixel and similar tools to measure ad performance, build custom and lookalike audiences, and understand site usage. These tools may set cookies or use similar identifiers in your browser. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your data for ads through your Meta ad preferences. Disabling cookies may affect how our Sites function.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Text messages: Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for assistance.
  • Marketing communications: You can decline the marketing consent checkbox at signup, or contact us at any time to opt out.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: You may contact us using the details in Section 12 to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and our legitimate business and legal recordkeeping needs.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Sites and services are directed to businesses and adults, and are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, industries served, or legal requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
